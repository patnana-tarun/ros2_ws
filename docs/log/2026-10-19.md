# 2026-10-19

## Done
- Demo fixes on `exp/tunnel-tracking` (e7c2822, 256fe8f): NavFn `allow_unknown: false` +
  `/map_explored`, 2-strike pre-check, goal hand-over 0.5 m, Spin removed then restored, sim
  track 0.34.
- `explore_coordinator` `stall_timeout` / `stall_radius` (off by default): gives up on a goal when
  the robot moved less than 0.3 m in 30 s (3a7ace6, `exp/tunnel-tracking`).
- Full comparison, 9 GUI runs: [[Full comparison 2026-10-19]]. Raw data is kept in
  `~/full_runs_2026-10-19/`, because the scratch folder holding the 2026-10-18 raw runs was wiped
  overnight. Their summaries, figures and scripts had been archived in `assets/`.

## Verified
- [[Navigation fixes 2026-10-19]]: root causes measured. After the fixes (round 2, 3 GUI runs): 0
  rotation events (before 2.7 per run), 3.2 % large open-area deviations (before 7.1 %), no path
  through unknown space longer than 0.23 m at plan time.
- 7 of 9 runs reached 90 % coverage. The other two stopped stuck above 75 % (86 %, 80 %).
- Tunnel heading deviation, p90: tuned 13.3° vs base 28.0° (this test). Pooled over 7 runs per
  setup, p = 0.006.
- Smooth reached 90 % fastest (9.1 min vs 14.6 min base) and had 89 % coverage at 10 min vs 73 %.
- The stall give-up loaded and logged in every run, and cut the median time lost per given-up goal
  from 60 s to 46–49 s.

## Not yet verified
- Which demo change causes the stuck regression (stuck 7 % → 33 %, goal success 59 % → 39 %).
- Whether a `wz_std` between 0.2 and 0.3 keeps tuned's straightness and smooth's speed.
- The exploration differences, which are consistent but not significant with 3 runs per setup.

## Found
- Removing Spin made stalls worse, and the rotation events weren't caused by it (my first
  attribution was a correlation). Reverted.
- The sim over-rotation (1.2–1.3×) is stick-slip: track width 0.43 and 0.34 give the same steady
  turn rates.
- The first smoke test crashed the coordinator: a new method `robot_xy()` was hidden by the
  existing attribute `self.robot_xy`. Renamed to `robot_position()` before any measured run.
