# 2026-10-28

## Done
- **Real-robot split: the Pi is the brain, the ESP32 is the controller** ([[explorer_bringup]]).
  The ESP32 now runs the motors, the 4 encoders, the per-side speed loop, the ICM-20948 IMU and
  the safety stop; the Pi runs ROS. Built from the user's `~/Downloads/rover_tests.zip` (sketches
  1–7: motors, IMU calibration, encoders, PID, heading, odometry, full system) and the
  register-level `icm20948_i2c.ino`, kept to their pins and gains.
- New firmware `src/explorer_bringup/firmware/explorer_esp32/`: `config.h`, `motors.h`,
  `encoders.h`, `imu.h`, `pilink.h` (line protocol with checksums), `explorer_esp32.ino`.
  Reads the accelerometer calibration that `icm20948_i2c.ino` saved in flash.
- New Pi nodes: `esp32_bridge` (replaces `explorer_base` and `mpu9250_imu`, which drove the Pi's
  GPIO and I²C; removed, in git history) and `x2_lidar`, a pure-Python YDLIDAR X2 driver (the
  vendor SDK is not packaged for Jazzy and was not installed). `robot.launch.py` gained
  `esp_port`, `lidar` (`x2` | `ydlidar` | `none`) and `lidar_port`; `robot.yaml` rewritten.
- Fusion stays in the EKF ([[Odometry fusion]]); the bridge adds a slip check that raises the
  wheel vx variance when a side's front and rear wheels disagree.
- Docs: [[explorer_bringup]] rewritten (split, firmware, wiring, bring-up steps), [[Robot model]]
  sensors, [[Topics]], [[Odometry fusion]], [[Known limitations]] #16, CLAUDE.md.

## Verified
- Firmware compiles with ESP32 Arduino core 3.3.12 (`arduino-cli`, `--warnings all`, none), in
  both encoder modes (quadrature, and single-channel with `TICKS_PER_REV` set): 336 kB flash,
  24 kB RAM.
- `robot.launch.py mock:=true`: `/wheel/odom`, `/imu`, `/joint_states` at 100 Hz, `/odom` at
  30 Hz. Commanded 0.15 m/s and 0.3 rad/s came back from the EKF as 0.150 and 0.301, the yaw
  rate taken from the gyro through the URDF's `imu_joint` rotation; vx 0 after `/cmd_vel` stops.
- `esp32_bridge` against a simulated ESP32 speaking the firmware's protocol: ~48 Hz, 440 of 440
  commands with valid checksums, vx 0.200 with variance 0.002, slip on one wheel → variance
  0.25, target 0 after `/cmd_vel` stops, reconnects after the link drops.
- `x2_lidar` against simulated X2 packets (with a corrupt packet every turn): 7.00 Hz, 360
  beams, 2 empty; an object at the X2's clockwise 80–100° shows at ROS −100 to −82° (the
  robot's right); SLAM Toolbox with `nav2_params.yaml` built an 80 × 79 map of the 4 m room, TF
  `map → laser_frame` resolved.
- `esp_port`/`lidar_port` given to a parent launch reach `robot.launch.py` through the include.

## Not yet verified
- Nothing has run on the hardware: the ESP32, motors, encoders, IMU and X2 are untested with
  this code. `TICKS_PER_REV` is still 0 (motors disabled) and the PI gains are rover_tests'
  starting values.
- The ICM-20948's mounting against the URDF's `imu_joint` (written for the MPU-9250, chip down).
  A wrong mounting flips the yaw rate; check step 4 in [[explorer_bringup]].
- The X2's zero direction (`angle_offset_deg`) and whether DTR high starts its motor.
