## Found
- Relay gaps with `/tf_static` and all-of-`/tf` forwarding (see [[Known limitations]] #1, #2).
- **Robot missing in Gazebo.** The Gazebo launch ran, but the robot never spawned: the spawner waited
  for `robot_description` forever. Cause: the `.bashrc` Fast DDS discovery server (192.168.123.1:11811)
  was not running, so local nodes could not discover each other. See [[Discovery server not running]].
- **Robot didn't move under teleop.** The Jazzy `turtlebot3_teleop` publishes `TwistStamped`, but the
  bridge subscribed to `Twist`. With mismatched types the bridge never connects, so no commands
  reached `DiffDrive`.
- **Robot flipped in the cave.** When the collision walls followed the sloped visual wall
  foot, the robot climbed them and flipped. Collision walls are now near-vertical.
- **Exploration port, what broke and why** (in order):
  1. SLAM leaves unknown speckles between X2 rays. They formed one frontier cluster centred on
     the robot, so it never moved. Fixed with `unknown_gap_fill` ([[Frontier exploration]]).
  2. The skid-steer turned at only ~35 % of the commanded rate, and Nav2's acceleration limit
     (relative to the measured rate) held turns near 0.1–0.26 rad/s. Fixed with the effective
     track width.
  3. Regulated Pure Pursuit flip-flopped its rotation with the goal directly behind. Switched
     to MPPI ([[Navigation stack]]).
  4. MPPI refused to start: `model_dt` must be ≥ 1 / `controller_frequency`.
  5. SLAM Toolbox activation through `nav2_bringup slam:=True` raced and failed once. SLAM is
     now started by the bringup under Nav2's lifecycle manager.
  6. Goals on frontier centroids next to walls took minutes at 0.25 m tolerance. Now 0.4 m.
  7. The 2D LiDAR can't see low rocks, so the robot got stuck on them (#14).
- **The demo video's "confused" robot** had three causes:
  1. Nav2 replanned every second, and the path around a rock flipped sides.
  2. Wall and rock inflation merged, so MPPI saw every trajectory as costly and dithered.
  3. The generator had put a stalagmite in the A→B tunnel.
- **Rated torque can't pivot.** With the motor's rated torque as the limit, the robot could
  not turn on the spot. The limit has to be the stall torque.
- **Bogie-first climbs worse.** In simulation it crossed one-sided rocks up to 6 cm, against
  8 cm for rocker-first ([[Rock climbing]]).
