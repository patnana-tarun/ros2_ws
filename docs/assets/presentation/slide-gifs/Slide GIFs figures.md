# Slide GIFs figures

Moving pictures for the Review 3 deck (Canva), recorded 2026-10-26 in Gazebo with the current
navigation (`tunnel_reverse.launch.py`, nav-speed config) through the rover tools. GIFs loop; the MP4s
are the same clips, smaller and sharper, for Canva's video element.

- `slide12_chase_cam_cave.gif` / `.mp4` — chase camera behind the rover driving across a chamber,
  past a boulder, into a tunnel (16 s).
- `slide18_frontier_selection.gif` — RViz around the rover: frontier candidates (dots, green = chosen)
  and the chosen goal (red arrow) jump to new frontiers as the map grows, 33 % → 52 % explored (30 s).
- `slide21_map_filling.gif` / `.mp4` — whole-cave RViz view, the map filling in from the start to
  95 % explored; 480 s of sim in 20 s, finished map held 3 s.
- `slide21_route_on_map.gif` — top-down: the rover's route drawn over the finished map for the same
  run (green forward, red reverse, orange turns on the spot); 95.3 % at 570 s.
- `slide22_reverse_out_map.gif` — top-down, close up: the rover drives into a dead end, backs out
  (red), turns on the spot (orange) and drives on.
- `slide22_reverse_out_chase_cam.gif` / `.mp4` — the same moment from the chase camera (14 s).
- `slide24_rotate_then_drive.gif` / `.mp4` — chase camera at the start: the first goal is behind the
  rover, so it turns on the spot to face the path, then drives (12 s).
